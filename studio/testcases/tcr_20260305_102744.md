## 📄 Dataset-kuvaus

The dataset primarily covers military tactics and strategies employed by the Russian Armed Forces, specifically detailed in the Army Techniques Publication ATP 7-100.1. The knowledge graph includes various entities such as military organizations (e.g., Caspian Sea Flotilla, Spetsnaz), concepts (e.g., Tactical Actions, Heavy Weapons), methods (e.g., Counterattacks, Airborne Assault), and artifacts (e.g., TOS-1 Complex, 2S19 Howitzer), along with their interrelationships. This data is typically used for military training, strategic planning, and analysis of operational capabilities, providing insights into Russian military doctrine and practices. Analytical questions that could be addressed include the effectiveness of specific tactics in various combat scenarios, comparisons of Russian military capabilities against adversaries, and the impact of technological advancements on military operations.
